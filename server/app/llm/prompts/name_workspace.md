# What to return

`name`: a short title for this collection.

# Rules

1. **Three to five words.** It sits in a header beside the document count, not on a book
   cover.
2. **Name what is in the collection**, not what the product does. "Acme invoices, Q1 2026"
   is a name; "Document Analysis Workspace" is a category.
3. **Use the words the documents use.** If they name a vendor, a customer or a period, that
   is what makes this collection findable later.
4. **No quotation marks, no trailing full stop, no emoji.**
5. If the documents have nothing in common, say what they are: "Ten mixed business
   documents" is more useful than a theme you had to invent.
